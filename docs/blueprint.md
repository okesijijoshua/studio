# **App Name**: LuxeScents

## Core Features:

- Navigation Bar: Sticky navigation bar with brand logo, navigation links (Home, Collections, About Us), and cart icon. Includes a responsive hamburger menu for mobile devices.
- Homepage: Homepage with a hero section, 'Peak at Our Collection' section (grid of 6 product cards with 'View Full Collection' button), testimonial section (horizontally scrollable on mobile), currency selector, and footer.
- AI Perfume Recommendation: AI tool perfume recommendation section that suggests perfumes based on simple rules or previously viewed items.  Displays 2-3 personalized recommendations.
- Collections Page: Collections page displaying products in a grid layout with pagination, filters (sort by price, alphabetically, brand, scent category, price range slider), and currency selection dropdown.
- About Us Page: About Us page with a business description, social media icon buttons, and testimonial cards.
- Shopping Cart: Shopping cart to view added items, update quantities, remove items, and display total cost based on selected currency.
- Currency Converter: Currency selection feature to select a currency from a dropdown and convert prices dynamically on the homepage and product cards. Uses localStorage for persistence. Supported currencies: USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, CNY, INR, CAD, AUD, NGN (default).

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Muted lavender (#D0B7D6) to evoke a sense of luxury and sophistication.
- Background color: Very light grey (#F5F5F5), almost white, to provide a clean and minimalist backdrop that keeps the focus on the products.
- Accent color: Soft rose (#F0D0CE) to complement the lavender and add a touch of femininity without being overpowering.
- Body text: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for body, providing a modern yet readable feel, paired with 'Playfair' (serif) for headlines to add elegance.
- Use minimalist, line-based icons for navigation and UI elements.
- Grid-based layout with generous spacing and padding to create a clean and airy feel. Responsive design to adapt to different screen sizes.
- Subtle fade-in animations on page load and transitions to enhance user experience without being distracting.
